How to use it

It runs in the browser, on a phone too. Drag to orbit, pinch or scroll to zoom.

  1. Choose an assembly at the top. It pulls apart. You can also tap a glowing assembly on the car. Where there's a torque, the label carries it. The Front brake and Battery shortcuts remain, and Assemble puts the selected assembly back together.
  2. Tap a part or its label. Its card has the torque, the specs, the part numbers (each with an FCP Euro search and the other MINIs that use it), and the pins and wire colours for anything wired. On a phone the card is a sheet along the bottom; tap its handle to make it taller.
  3. Right-click a part, or hold it on a phone, to see where it sits on the car. It goes back in place with what it bolts to, everything further out stays pulled away, and the same right-click, hold or Back pulls it apart again.
  4. Wiring draws the diagrams in the wires' real colours. Tap a pin in the table and its wire lights up on the diagram; tap a wire and its pin lights up in the table.
  5. Back, Esc or the browser's back button step out one view at a time. Back to the post at the top left comes back here.

The 3D

The parts are modelled for now, and so is the F56 body around them.

Every part goes through the same hologram look: a see-through fill that glows at its edges, scanlines and bright outlines. Modelled or scanned, they render the same, so 3D scans of the real parts can swap in one at a time without anything looking out of place.

Where it's at

  • Disc size. Two sizes fit the F56, 280 x 22 and 294 x 22 mm. The model draws 294 until I measure mine.
  • DSC unit. A look at the connector settles 38-pin against 46-pin.
  • Model fit. The new exterior shapes and mounting positions are approximate. They're for exploring the assemblies, not making replacement parts. Small seats and bores still need refinement.
  • Equipment. The body uses the standard Cooper S bumper reference. The actual bumper, headlight technology and optional front modules need checking against the car; alternatives on the cards are not all installed together.
  • Small fittings. Hose routes, small mounting seats and the fan-relay location still need comparison with the car.
