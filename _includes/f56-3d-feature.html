How to use it

It runs in the browser, on a phone too. Drag to orbit, pinch or scroll to zoom.

  1. Tap the driver's front wheel. The camera flies into the front left corner.
  2. Tap Exploded. The brake comes apart along the hub, wheel first and knuckle last. Where there's a torque, the label carries it.
  3. Tap a part or its label. Its card has the torque, the specs, the DSC pins for the two sensors and every repair step that mentions the part, photo included. Tap a photo for full size.
  4. Wiring opens the DSC diagrams with the part's pins lit up. Procedures has all seven repair procedures in full.
  5. Back, Esc or the browser's back button step out one view at a time. Back to the post at the top left comes back here.

The 3D

The parts are modelled for now, and the car body is a placeholder shell.

Every part goes through the same hologram look: a see-through fill that glows at its edges, scanlines and bright outlines. Modelled or scanned, they render the same, so 3D scans of the real parts can swap in one at a time without anything looking out of place.

Where it's at

  • Disc size. Two sizes fit the F56, 280 x 22 and 294 x 22 mm. The model draws 294 until I measure mine.
  • DSC unit. A look at the connector settles 38-pin against 46-pin.
  • Wheel speed sensor. It's in the model with its DSC pins, but no repair steps cover it yet.
  • The rest of the car. This corner is the test. If it holds up, the other systems follow the same way.
