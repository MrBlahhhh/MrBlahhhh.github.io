How to use it

It runs in the browser, on a phone too. Drag to orbit, pinch or scroll to zoom.

  1. Tap the driver's front wheel. The camera flies into the front left corner.
  2. Tap Exploded. The brake comes apart along the hub, wheel first and knuckle last. Where ISTA gives a torque, the label carries it.
  3. Tap a part or its label. Its card has the torque, the technical data, the DSC pins for the two sensors and every ISTA repair step that mentions the part, photo included. Tap a photo for full size.
  4. Wiring opens BMW's DSC diagrams with the part's pins lit up. ISTA procedures has all seven repair documents in full.
  5. Back, Esc or the browser's back button step out one view at a time. Back to the post at the top left comes back here.

Where it comes from

Everything you read in it is BMW's, from ISTA 4.39.20, the dealer workshop system. Nothing is typed in by hand.

Picked for this car

ISTA tags every step, photo and torque with rules on model, engine, market and equipment. The extractor runs them against my car's type code, XP83: Cooper S, B48, automatic, US. ISTA files the US B48 as a B46.

Torques

The front brake table: disc to hub 16 Nm, caliper to knuckle 110 Nm, guide bolts 35 Nm, protective plate 12 Nm.

Technical data

Disc sizes, the 0.01 mm thickness variation limit, minimum disc thickness and the pad wear warning.

Wiring

ISTA's own diagrams and pin tables. The F56 has two DSC units: a 46-pin one that also runs an electric parking brake, and a 38-pin one. Mine has the cable handbrake, so it's most likely the 38-pin, and the explorer shows that one first.

The 3D

The parts are modelled for now: the disc to ISTA's size, the rest by eye from ISTA's photos. The car body is a placeholder shell.

Every part goes through the same hologram look: a see-through fill that glows at its edges, scanlines and bright outlines. Modelled or scanned, they render the same, so 3D scans of the real parts can swap in one at a time without anything looking out of place.

Where it's at

  • Disc size. ISTA lists 280 x 22 and 294 x 22 mm for the F56 and says to go by the VIN. The model draws 294 until I measure mine.
  • DSC unit. A look at the connector settles 38-pin against 46-pin.
  • Wheel speed sensor. It's in the model with its DSC pins, but none of the procedures pulled out so far cover it.
  • The rest of the car. This corner is the test. If it holds up, the other systems follow the same way.
