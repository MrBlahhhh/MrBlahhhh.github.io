R53 JARVIS · PUBLIC DEMO

A front corner you can pull apart.

This is the first public R53 Jarvis demo. It opens on the driver's front wheel and brake assembly, with a factory-style 17-inch R85 eight-spoke wheel. Rotate it, separate the parts, and tap a component to inspect it.

No account or app purchase is needed to try this demo.

Open the R53 demo ↗How to use it ↓

Try the corner

  1. Rotate and zoom. Drag the model to orbit. Scroll to zoom, or pinch on a phone.
  2. Pull it apart. Choose Explode to separate the wheel, brake and suspension groups.
  3. Inspect a part. Click or tap a label or shape. The card names the part and shows confirmed replacement references where available.
  4. Put it together. Use Back or Esc, then Assemble. The separate full-screen link is useful on a phone.

What is modelled

The wheel has curved spokes, a shaped barrel, open bolt holes and a separate tyre. Behind it are the ventilated disc and hat, four-bolt hub, dust shield, caliper carrier, pads, piston, guide hardware and brake hose. The knuckle, strut, spring and upper mount put the corner in context.

Reference geometry. This example uses a 7J × 17 ET48 wheel, a 205/45 R17 tyre and a 294 × 22 mm sports-brake example. It does not identify the brake kit on your own car. Shapes, casting details and positions remain illustrative; this is not measured manufacturing CAD. Confirm actual fitment before ordering.
