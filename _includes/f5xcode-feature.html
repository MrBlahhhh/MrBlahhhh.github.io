What it logs

Pick a preset, tap Start logging and drive. Each preset reads what that job needs as fast as the adapter allows, and opens on graphs drawn for it.

EngineTuning: boost and spark

RPM, boost and target, wastegate duty and position, MAF, load, timing on each cylinder, pedal, throttle and target, intake and charge air temps, lambda and target, trims, and high and low-side fuel pressure. Over ENET it all comes from the DME in one read a row. A cylinder whose timing drops below the others is the one pulling for knock.

EngineRough running and tip-in

RPM, pedal, throttle, load, MAP, timing, lambda and target and rail pressure every sweep, with trims, MAF, temps and the rear O2 behind them. Misfire counts per cylinder, and RPM falling while the pedal goes down is flagged as a dip.

Engine + gearboxStutter and shudder

Engine data alongside the six-speed automatic: engine, turbine and output speed, slip, gear and target gear, converter lockup, solenoid duty, current and pressure, ATF temperature and the torque the gearbox is asked for.

GearboxTransmission, fast

Transmission only, several rows a second: turbine and engine speed, slip and lockup state every row, with gear, solenoid, pedal, output speed and torque in turn. For watching the converter lock, slip and release around a shudder.

EngineCold start lean check

From a cold start through a few minutes of idle. Stretches where lambda runs 0.10 leaner than the DME's target are shaded, next to the DME's own misfire counters, rough running per cylinder, rail pressure against target and low-side pressure.

EngineFuel trims and vacuum leaks

Short, long and total trim, MAF, load, lambda and purge flow. A minute at idle and a minute at 2,500 rpm tells a vacuum leak from a fuel delivery problem.

EngineTemps and health

Coolant, oil, intake, ambient and cat temperature, module voltage, run time and fuel level, one row a second, so a long drive stays a small file.

The graphs

  • Replay any log on one time axis, pinch to zoom, and read every channel at the point you tap.
  • Lambda as AFR by default, with fuel cut shaded and lean stretches you can jump between.
  • Marks. Tap Mark when the car does the thing, and the mark is on every chart.
  • Rough running, all cylinders: each cylinder's misfire increases with RPM, load, boost and rail pressure underneath.
  • Screen off. Recording carries on with the phone locked, with Mark and Stop on the notification.
  • Share any log as CSV to Drive, Files or a message, or send it to me from the app.

What else it does

Fault codes, oil level and module coding, on the same connection as the logger. The engine's fault codes are free; the rest is the $35 unlock.

Fault codes in plain English

Every module's faults, each with whether it's happening now or stored, whether it lights the dash, what it means, the likely causes and what to check first. Check all fault codes reads every module in one pass, so a check engine light can't hide in a module you didn't pick. Clear them one module at a time.

Oil level

The engine's own oil level sensor as a gauge, from a quart low to half a quart over. Save your own full mark, idling warm on level ground.

Read and back up coding

Every setting in a module by name, in Popular, All and Expert views. A backup is saved before anything changes, and backups export and import between phones.

Change coding

Pick a setting, choose the value, review it and save to the car. Each save checks the module's identity and signature, writes, then reads it back to prove it landed.

Compare and history

Compare any backup with your original, setting by setting, and see every save, restore and check you've made. Restore the original the same way it was saved.

Body module recovery

Back up the body module in any state and put a saved backup back unchanged, for a module left half-written by an interrupted session.

Coding covers eleven modules: airbag, engine, transmission, stability control, steering, active sound, body, instrument cluster, head unit, controller and climate.

What you need

An Android phone and one of these adapters. ENET is the fastest, and it's what the Tuning preset needs to read the DME's full channel set.

fastestENET cable

Into a USB-C Ethernet adapter on the phone. The app finds the car's gateway on its own.

fastestMHD Wi-Fi adapter

The phone joins the adapter's Wi-Fi and talks ENET through it.

Bluetooth or Wi-Fi OBD

OBDLink CX or MX+, Vgate iCar Pro 2S, or a Wi-Fi ELM327. Faults, coding and the gearbox presets work over these; Tuning falls back to standard OBD channels.

Android 7 or newer. Keep the car on a battery charger for coding: a module that restarts partway through a write means low voltage.

What it can and can't do

It reads, logs and codes. It doesn't flash or tune.

it can
  • Read and clear fault codes on every module, in English.
  • Log the engine, misfires and the six-speed automatic, and graph it.
  • Read the oil level from the engine's own sensor.
  • Back up, change and restore coding on eleven modules, with a signature check and read-back on every save.
it can't
  • Flash or tune engine software.
  • Change coding on module software it hasn't been checked against. Those modules can still be read and backed up. More are added with each update.
  • Run on iPhone. Android only.
